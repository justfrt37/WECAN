Before you write

Plumm has no accounts, so we cannot look you up by name or email. If your question is about your data, your coins or a specific conversation, please include your anonymous ID: open the app → Profile tab → tap the ID under “Guest User” to copy it.

Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Manage or cancel them in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Coins

Coins are included with each subscription period and renew every period. If a purchase did not arrive, open the app, pull the Profile tab to refresh, and if it still hasn't landed email us with your anonymous ID and the approximate time of purchase.

Delete your data

Deleting a conversation in the app deletes it on the server too. To erase everything tied to your anonymous session, email us that ID and we will action it within 30 days.

Report content or a safety concern

All characters are fictional and AI-generated. If a reply crosses a line, or you believe a character or image is inappropriate, email us with a screenshot and the character name — we review every report.
