## Description (max 4,000)

Plumm is your private AI companion. Swipe through a world of characters, match with the one who catches your eye, and start a conversation that never runs out — she remembers what you told her yesterday and picks the thread back up today.

DISCOVER AND MATCH
• Swipe through dozens of distinct personalities — sweet, playful, distant, devoted, teasing
• Realistic or illustrated art styles, from every corner of the world
• See who liked you back and open the chat whenever you're ready
• Every character has her own job, interests, mood and daily rhythm

A CONVERSATION THAT FEELS ALIVE
• She writes like a person, not a chatbot — short, natural, in your language
• Long-term memory: names, plans, inside jokes, the things you only mentioned once
• She reacts to the photos you send, and sends photos of her own
• Voice messages in her own voice, whenever you want to hear her (Pro+ and Max)

REAL-TIME VOICE CALLS (Pro+ and Max)
Tap the phone icon and talk out loud. She listens, answers in her voice, and you can cut in mid-sentence — it flows like a real call, not a walkie-talkie.

BUILD THE RELATIONSHIP
Ten relationship levels, from strangers to inseparable. Every conversation moves you forward, unlocks a warmer version of her and changes the way she talks to you.

CREATE YOUR OWN (paid plans)
Choose her look, her age, her ethnicity, her style, her profession, her interests — even the history the two of you share. Our AI generates her photo and her personality, and she is yours alone.

YOUR OWN GALLERY
Every photo she sends you is kept in her gallery, next to the ones you sent her. Scroll back through the whole story any time.

SHE HAS A LIFE OF HER OWN
Characters keep their own daily rhythm — working, out with friends, asleep at night. Write while she is busy and she answers later, the way a real person would. Go quiet for a few days and you may find a message waiting for you.

COME BACK EVERY DAY
Daily rewards give you free tokens just for showing up, and the streak grows the longer you keep it alive.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No account, no email, no phone number — you are anonymous from the first launch
• No ads, no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics, no tracking prompt
• Your conversations are protected at the database level by Row Level Security, so no other user can reach them
• The name you enter and the photos you send stay on your device
• Delete any conversation from inside the app and it is deleted on the server too

Every character in Plumm is fictional and generated by artificial intelligence.

SUBSCRIPTION
Plumm is free to download. Chatting runs on tokens, and a daily reward tops you up every time you open the app — the longer your streak, the bigger the reward. Three plans go further:
• Pro — a large recurring token balance and one custom character per week
• Pro+ — everything in Pro, plus voice messages, real-time voice calls and up to five custom characters per week
• Max — the largest token balance, voice, and unlimited custom characters

Each plan is available weekly, monthly or yearly, and one-time token packs can be bought on their own without any subscription.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Your subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app and vary by region.

## Notes before you paste

1. The two legal URLs above must actually resolve. `docs/legal/privacy.html` and `docs/legal/terms.html` are ready in this repo — host them at those paths (or change the links to wherever you host them). `docs/appstore/support.html` is a ready-made support page for the Support URL.
2. Apple requires the Terms of Use (EULA) link and the auto-renew disclosure in the description whenever you sell auto-renewable subscriptions — both are included above.
3. Keep the age rating at 17+/18+ and the "Frequent/Intense Mature Themes" answers consistent with this copy.
4. Avoid adding words like "no filter", "NSFW", "uncensored" or "nudes" to metadata — App Review reads metadata first, and that vocabulary is the fastest way to a rejection for an app in this category.
5. Do not add user-count or rating claims ("1,000,000+ users", "4.9 stars") to the description unless you can back them up; the in-app onboarding screen is marketing, App Store metadata is held to a higher bar.
6. Keywords deliberately do NOT repeat "ai", "dating" or "app" — those are already in the app name, and Apple indexes name + keywords together and builds the combinations itself. Repeating them wastes characters.
